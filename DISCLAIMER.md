The initial reference price is a planning input only. It is not a promise of market value, a valuation opinion, an offer, or an invitation to buy or sell an asset.

## DEX and liquidity integrations

Jupiter, Raydium, Meteora, and Orca are independent third-party protocols. PowerChain does not control their programs, routing, liquidity, pricing, fees, availability, or security. A displayed quote is informational until a wallet signs and the transaction is finalized. Token-2022 transfer fees can change the amount received and may not be supported uniformly by every route, pool type, wallet, custodian, or exchange.

## Payments

PowerPay reference code supports SOL, USDC, and approved Token-2022 assets only after explicit configuration and validation. Payment requests do not guarantee settlement. Applications must independently verify mint addresses, token programs, amounts, recipient accounts, signatures, blockhash validity, finality, and refund policy.

## No custody or key recovery

The software must not collect seed phrases or private keys. Users remain responsible for wallets and transaction approvals. Production authorities must use governance-controlled multisig, timelocks, HSM/KMS, hardware wallets, or isolated signer services.
